Over a century of tradition, discipline and virtue

Founded by the De La Salle Brothers in 1915, SGI is one of the oldest schools in Malaysia, and home to generations of Old Georgians.

  1. 1914

    A school for Taiping

    Brother James Joseph Byrne proposes a school. The Kwa family donates the land.

  2. 1915

    Doors open

    SGI opens on 18 January.

  3. 1942

    The war years

    The school is closed during the Japanese Occupation.

  4. 1946

    Return and rebuild

    Classes return and rebuilding begins.

  5. 1965

    Golden jubilee

    SGI celebrates 50 years.

  6. 2015

    The centennial

    A parade and a homecoming dinner with over 1,400 Old Georgians.

  7. Today

    SMK St. George

    A landmark on the Taiping Heritage Trail.
